風，如何成為全世界成長最快的電力來源之一How wind became one of the world's fastest-growing sources of electricity

從 1888 年美國克里夫蘭一座 12 kW 的實驗風機，到 2025 年底全球約 1,287 GW 的裝置容量——風力發電用不到 140 年，從發明家的後院走進各國電網的核心。這一頁用 12 個章節，搭配本站的全球資料集與台灣即時資料，帶你看懂風電的歷史、技術、各國發展，以及台灣的位置。每一章都能直接跳到 3D 地球儀，在地圖上重播那段歷史。

From a 12 kW experimental machine in Cleveland in 1888 to about 1,287 GW installed worldwide by the end of 2025, wind power moved from inventors' backyards into the core of national grids in under 140 years. These twelve chapters, backed by this site's global dataset and Taiwan's live data, explain its history, its technology, the countries that built it — and where Taiwan stands. Every chapter links to the 3D globe so you can replay that part of the story on the map.

01 · 1980 – 2025

全球一覽：指數成長的 45 年At a glance: 45 years of exponential growth

全球風電累計裝置容量在 2023 年首度突破 1 太瓦（TW，即 1,000 GW），2025 年底約 1,287 GW。成長曲線是典型的指數型：全球在 1985 年跨過 1 GW、1999 年跨過 10 GW、2008 年跨過 100 GW，之後每幾年就再長一大截。2025 年單年新增約 157 GW，是有紀錄以來最多的一年。

Cumulative wind capacity passed 1 terawatt (TW, i.e. 1,000 GW) for the first time in 2023 and reached about 1,287 GW by the end of 2025. The curve is classically exponential: the world crossed 1 GW in 1985, 10 GW in 1999 and 100 GW in 2008, and has grown by huge steps every few years since. About 157 GW was added in 2025 alone — the largest annual addition on record.

絕大多數容量仍在陸地上；離岸風電只佔約 7%（約 92 GW），卻是近十年技術變化最大、成長最受矚目的領域。下圖是本站資料集中 79 個國家的年底累計值加總——把滑鼠移到圖上（或用方向鍵）可看每一年的數字，也可以切換成表格。

Most capacity is still on land. Offshore wind is only about 7% of the total (roughly 92 GW), yet it has been the most technologically dynamic part of the industry over the past decade. The chart sums year-end capacity for the 79 countries in this site's dataset — hover (or use the arrow keys) for each year's values, or switch to the table view.

容量不等於發電量。裝置容量（MW、GW）是風機「最多能發多少電」；實際發電量（度、kWh）還要看風。風場一年平均大約能發出裝置容量 25–50% 的電（容量因數），離岸通常比陸域高。Capacity is not generation. Installed capacity (MW, GW) is the most a turbine can produce; the energy actually generated (kWh) depends on the wind. Over a year a wind farm typically delivers 25–50% of its capacity (the capacity factor), offshore usually at the higher end.

02 · 1888 – 1979

起源：從發明家的後院到石油危機Origins: from an inventor's backyard to the oil crises

用風發電的想法，幾乎和電力工業一樣早。1888 年，美國發明家 Charles Brush 在克里夫蘭自家後院蓋了一座葉輪直徑 17 公尺、144 片木製葉片的風機，以 12 kW 的發電機替地下室的蓄電池充電，一般認為是第一部自動運轉的發電用風機。1890 年代，丹麥的 Poul la Cour 在阿斯科夫（Askov）用風洞實驗證明：葉片少、轉得快的風機，發電效率遠比葉片多、轉得慢的好。

The idea of making electricity from wind is almost as old as the power industry. In 1888 the American inventor Charles Brush built a 17-metre, 144-blade wooden rotor behind his house in Cleveland; its 12 kW dynamo charged the batteries in his basement, and it is generally regarded as the first automatically operated wind turbine for electricity. In the 1890s the Dane Poul la Cour used wind-tunnel experiments at Askov to show that fast-spinning rotors with few blades generate electricity far more efficiently than slow ones with many.

接下來半個世紀出現幾部重要的大型試驗機：1931 年蘇聯在克里米亞雅爾達附近的 Balaklava 建造 100 kW 的 D-30，並接上當地電網；1941 年美國佛蒙特州 Grandpa's Knob 的 Smith–Putnam 風機達到 1.25 MW，是第一部百萬瓦級風機，可惜 1945 年葉片斷裂後停止運轉。1957 年，丹麥工程師 Johannes Juul 的 Gedser 風機（200 kW）結合「三葉片、迎風、失速控制」，成為日後「丹麥式風機」的原型。

Several large experimental machines followed. In 1931 the Soviet 100 kW D-30 at Balaklava near Yalta fed a local grid; in 1941 the Smith–Putnam turbine on Grandpa's Knob, Vermont, reached 1.25 MW — the first megawatt-class machine — until a blade failed in 1945. In 1957 Johannes Juul's 200 kW Gedser turbine in Denmark combined three blades, an upwind rotor and stall control: the template for the later "Danish concept".

1973 與 1979 年兩次石油危機，讓各國重新重視風能。美國航太總署（NASA）與能源部推出 MOD 系列大型試驗機（1975 年的 MOD-0、1979 年的 2 MW MOD-1），德國也在 1983 年建造葉輪直徑 100 公尺的 3 MW 巨型機 Growian。這些由政府主導、一步到位的大型機大多問題不斷、很快退場；反而是丹麥的農機廠商從幾十 kW 的小風機一步步放大，靠實際運轉經驗累積可靠度——這條「由小做大」的路線，最後贏得了市場。1978 年由學校師生志工自建的丹麥 Tvind 風機（2 MW）至今仍在運轉。

The oil crises of 1973 and 1979 revived interest everywhere. NASA and the US Department of Energy built the MOD series of large test machines (the MOD-0 in 1975, the 2 MW MOD-1 in 1979), and Germany built Growian, a 3 MW giant with a 100-metre rotor, in 1983. Most of these state-led, leap-to-the-top prototypes struggled and were soon retired. Instead, Danish agricultural-machinery makers scaled up step by step from turbines of a few dozen kilowatts, building reliability through field experience — and that bottom-up path won the market. The 2 MW Tvind turbine, built by teachers and students in 1978, still turns today.

03 · 1980 – 1999

起飛：加州風潮與丹麥設計Take-off: the California wind rush and the Danish design

1980 年，美國新罕布夏州的 Crotched Mountain 立起 20 部 30 kW 風機，常被視為世界第一座風場。真正的爆發在加州：聯邦與州政府的租稅優惠，讓 Altamont Pass、Tehachapi、San Gorgonio 等山口在幾年內插滿數千部小型風機，史稱「加州風潮」（California wind rush）。1985 年全球風電首度超過 1 GW，其中九成以上在美國、主要在加州。

In 1980 twenty 30 kW turbines went up on Crotched Mountain in New Hampshire, often called the world's first wind farm. The real boom came in California: federal and state tax incentives filled mountain passes such as Altamont, Tehachapi and San Gorgonio with thousands of small turbines within a few years — the "California wind rush". In 1985 world wind capacity passed 1 GW for the first time, more than 90% of it in the United States, mostly in California.

加州風場裡有大量進口的丹麥風機——Vestas、Bonus、Nordtank、Micon 等廠商靠「丹麥式」設計的耐用度，在這裡建立出口事業。1980 年代中期優惠陸續到期後，美國市場急凍、許多廠商倒閉，全球風電的重心轉回歐洲。

Many of those Californian machines were imported from Denmark: Vestas, Bonus, Nordtank, Micon and others built an export business on the durability of the "Danish concept". When the incentives expired in the mid-1980s the US market collapsed, many manufacturers went bankrupt, and the centre of gravity moved back to Europe.

1990 年代由歐洲的政策接手：德國 1991 年通過《電力饋網法》（Stromeinspeisungsgesetz），保證風電以固定價格售電；丹麥、西班牙也推出各自的收購制度。單機容量在這十年從幾百 kW 走到百萬瓦級（1995 年 Enercon E-66 1.5 MW、1997 年 Vestas V66 1.65 MW）。依本站資料，德國在 1997 年超越美國成為全球累計容量第一；1999 年全球風電跨過 10 GW。

In the 1990s European policy took over. Germany's 1991 Electricity Feed-in Act (Stromeinspeisungsgesetz) guaranteed wind power a fixed price, and Denmark and Spain introduced their own purchase schemes. Turbines grew from a few hundred kilowatts to the megawatt class during the decade (Enercon's E-66 at 1.5 MW in 1995, Vestas' V66 at 1.65 MW in 1997). In this site's data Germany overtook the US as the largest wind country in 1997, and the world passed 10 GW in 1999.

同一時期，印度 1986 年在坦米爾納德邦的 Muppandal 展開第一期風場；中國 1990 年代在新疆達坂城發展風電（今日金風科技的源頭）；1991 年，丹麥 Vindeby 立起世界第一座離岸風場。這些「第一次」，在接下來的二十年都長成了巨大的產業。

Meanwhile India opened the first phase of Muppandal in Tamil Nadu in 1986, China began developing wind at Dabancheng in Xinjiang in the 1990s (the roots of today's Goldwind), and in 1991 Denmark's Vindeby became the world's first offshore wind farm. Each of these "firsts" grew into a huge industry over the next two decades.

04 · 2000 – 2010

擴張：政策推動的規模化Scale-up: policy-driven growth

2000 年，德國以《再生能源法》（EEG）取代舊法，以 20 年保證收購價格與優先併網吸引大量投資；德國在 1997–2007 年間一直是全球風電第一大國。西班牙在這十年成長將近十倍。美國則在聯邦「生產稅額抵減」（PTC）時有時無的延長之間，走出一段段繁榮與停頓，並在 2008 年重回全球第一。

In 2000 Germany replaced its old law with the Renewable Energy Sources Act (EEG), which guaranteed purchase prices for 20 years and priority grid access and drew in a wave of investment; Germany had the world's largest wind fleet from 1997 to 2007. Spain grew almost tenfold during the decade. The United States went through boom-and-bust cycles as the federal Production Tax Credit (PTC) lapsed and was renewed, and regained first place in 2008.

中國在 2005 年通過《可再生能源法》（2006 年施行），並規劃大型風電基地，裝置容量從 2005 年的約 1 GW 衝到 2010 年的近 30 GW；2009 年啟動的甘肅酒泉風電基地，規劃規模達千萬瓩（10 GW）等級。

China passed its Renewable Energy Law in 2005 (in force from 2006) and planned giant wind bases; its capacity rose from about 1 GW in 2005 to nearly 30 GW in 2010. The Jiuquan wind base in Gansu, launched in 2009, was planned at the 10 GW scale.

風機也越做越大：2007 年 Enercon E-126 單機達 7.58 MW、葉輪直徑 127 公尺。全球風電在 2008 年突破 100 GW，2010 年接近 181 GW；本資料集中有風電的國家，從 2000 年的 43 國增加到 2010 年的 65 國。

Turbines kept growing: in 2007 Enercon's E-126 reached 7.58 MW with a 127-metre rotor. World capacity passed 100 GW in 2008 and approached 181 GW in 2010, and the number of countries with wind power in this dataset rose from 43 in 2000 to 65 in 2010.

05 · 2010 – 2025

亞洲世紀：風電重心東移Asia's century: the centre of gravity moves east

2010 年之後，風電的重心明顯東移。依本站採用的 IRENA 併網口徑，中國在 2011 年超越美國成為全球第一（若依 GWEC 的吊裝口徑，則是 2010 年），之後差距越拉越大；2025 年中國累計約 641 GW，接近全球的一半。

After 2010 the centre of gravity moved decisively east. On the grid-connected IRENA basis used here, China overtook the United States in 2011 (on GWEC's installed basis, in 2010) and has pulled further ahead every year since; by 2025 China had about 641 GW, close to half of the world total.

以洲別來看，2000 年歐洲佔全球風電約 75%、亞洲只有 8%；亞洲在 2015 年超越歐洲，2025 年已佔 57%，歐洲降到 22%、北美約 14%。印度（約 55 GW，全球第 4）與巴西（約 35 GW，第 5）也躋身前五大，拉丁美洲、非洲與中東的新興市場逐年擴大。

By continent, Europe held about 75% of the world's wind capacity in 2000 and Asia just 8%. Asia overtook Europe in 2015 and reached 57% in 2025, with Europe down to 22% and North America at about 14%. India (about 55 GW, fourth) and Brazil (about 35 GW, fifth) joined the top five, and emerging markets in Latin America, Africa and the Middle East keep growing.

2023 年全球累計風電首度超過 1 TW；2025 年單年新增約 157 GW，創歷史新高。2015 到 2025 年間，全球風電容量成長約 3.1 倍；前五大國家（中、美、德、印、巴）合計約佔全球 75%。

Global capacity passed 1 TW in 2023, and about 157 GW was added in 2025, a record. Between 2015 and 2025 the world's wind capacity grew about 3.1-fold, and the top five countries (China, the US, Germany, India and Brazil) together hold about 75% of it.

各國數字會因資料來源與口徑（吊裝、併網、完工）而不同。本站的國際比較統一採用 IRENA 年底累計值，並把陸域與離岸分開；詳見第 12 章「資料來源與方法」。Country figures differ by source and definition (installed, grid-connected, fully commissioned). International comparisons on this site use IRENA year-end totals split into onshore and offshore; see chapter 12, "Sources & method".

06 · 1991 – 2025

走向大海：離岸風電Going offshore

海上的風比陸地更強、更穩定，沒有山丘與建築阻擋；風機可以做得更大（不受道路運輸限制），又遠離住家。代價是基礎、海纜、施工船隊與維運都更昂貴，還得面對鹽霧、颱風與海浪等嚴苛環境。

Winds at sea are stronger and steadier than on land, with no hills or buildings in the way; turbines can be bigger (no road-transport limits) and sit far from homes. The price is costlier foundations, cables, installation vessels and maintenance, in a harsh environment of salt spray, storms and waves.

世界第一座離岸風場是 1991 年丹麥的 Vindeby：11 部 450 kW 風機、合計 4.95 MW，運轉超過 25 年才除役。2000 年哥本哈根外海的 Middelgrunden（20 部 2 MW）有一半由市民合作社持有；2002 年的 Horns Rev 1（80 部 2 MW、160 MW）則是第一座大型離岸風場，證明離岸風電可以規模化。

The world's first offshore wind farm was Denmark's Vindeby in 1991: eleven 450 kW turbines, 4.95 MW in total, which ran for more than 25 years before decommissioning. Middelgrunden off Copenhagen (twenty 2 MW turbines, 2000) was half owned by a citizens' cooperative, and Horns Rev 1 (eighty 2 MW turbines, 160 MW) in 2002 was the first large-scale offshore farm, proving that offshore wind could scale.

英國在 2008–2020 年間是全球離岸風電第一大國，靠差價合約（CfD）競標與大型開發區塊，陸續建成 London Array（630 MW，2013）、Hornsea 2（1.32 GW，2022）等巨型風場；2025 年英國約一半的風電容量在海上。2018 年荷蘭 Hollandse Kust Zuid 標案出現「零補貼」得標，象徵離岸風電成本大幅下降。2021 年起，中國成為離岸風電第一大國，2025 年約 48 GW，佔全球離岸容量一半以上。

The United Kingdom led offshore wind from 2008 to 2020; Contracts for Difference (CfD) auctions and large leasing rounds delivered giants such as London Array (630 MW, 2013) and Hornsea 2 (1.32 GW, 2022), and by 2025 about half of the UK's wind capacity was at sea. In 2018 the Dutch Hollandse Kust Zuid tender was won with zero subsidy, a sign of how far costs had fallen. Since 2021 China has been the largest offshore market, with about 48 GW in 2025 — more than half of the world's offshore capacity.

台灣海峽有強勁穩定的東北季風與相對淺的水深，是世界級的離岸風場址。依本站資料，台灣 2025 年底離岸風電約 3.6 GW，名列全球第 5，也是中國以外亞洲最大的離岸市場（見第 9 章）。

The Taiwan Strait combines a strong, steady winter monsoon with relatively shallow water, making it a world-class offshore site. In this site's data Taiwan had about 3.6 GW offshore at the end of 2025 — fifth in the world and the largest offshore market in Asia outside China (see chapter 9).

風機怎麼「站」在海上？How do turbines stand in the sea?

水深約 50–60 公尺以內多用固定式基礎：單樁（monopile，把一根巨大鋼管打入海床）最常見；套管式（jacket，三或四支腳的鋼構架）適合較深或地質較複雜的海域，台灣許多風場就採用套管式；此外還有重力式基礎。更深的海域則要改用浮動式平台（下一章）。

Up to roughly 50–60 m of water, turbines sit on fixed foundations: the monopile (one huge steel tube driven into the seabed) is the most common; the jacket (a three- or four-legged steel lattice) suits deeper water or harder ground and is used by many Taiwanese farms; gravity-based foundations also exist. Deeper still, turbines need floating platforms (next chapter).

07 · 2009 – 2025

漂浮的未來：浮動式風電A floating future

水深超過約 50–60 公尺，固定式基礎的成本會急遽上升。浮動式風電把風機裝在漂浮平台上，再以繫泊纜繩與錨固定在海床，讓風場可以走向更深、風況更好的海域——全球大多數的離岸風能資源都位於深水區。

Beyond roughly 50–60 m of water, fixed foundations quickly become too expensive. Floating wind mounts the turbine on a buoyant platform held by mooring lines and anchors, opening up deeper, windier waters — where most of the world's offshore wind resource lies.

挪威 Equinor 在 2009 年於 Karmøy 外海放下第一部大型浮動式試驗機 Hywind Demo（2.3 MW，柱式浮台）；2017 年的 Hywind Scotland（5 部 6 MW、30 MW）是世界第一座浮動式風場。之後有葡萄牙 WindFloat Atlantic（25 MW，2020，半潛式）、英國 Kincardine（約 50 MW，2021），以及 2023 年替北海油氣平台供電的挪威 Hywind Tampen（88 MW）。

Norway's Equinor launched the first large floating test turbine, Hywind Demo (2.3 MW on a spar buoy), off Karmøy in 2009; Hywind Scotland (five 6 MW turbines, 30 MW) became the world's first floating wind farm in 2017. WindFloat Atlantic in Portugal (25 MW, semi-submersible, 2020), Kincardine in the UK (about 50 MW, 2021) and Norway's Hywind Tampen (88 MW, 2023), which powers North Sea oil and gas platforms, followed.

浮台主要有三種：柱式（spar，細長如浮標、吃水很深）、半潛式（semi-submersible，三到四個浮筒）、張力腿式（TLP，以繃緊的纜繩往下拉住）。本資料集收錄的浮動式專案有 20 個，多半仍是示範或小型商業規模；成本還在下降中，被視為下一個十年的關鍵技術。台灣海峽的淺水場址有限，政府與業界也已開始評估更深海域的浮動式技術，作為下一階段的選項。

There are three main platform types: the spar (a long, deep-draught buoy), the semi-submersible (three or four columns) and the tension-leg platform (TLP, pulled down by taut tethers). The 20 floating projects in this dataset are still mostly demonstrators or small commercial arrays; costs are falling and floating wind is widely seen as a key technology for the next decade. With shallow sites in the Taiwan Strait limited, Taiwan's government and industry have also begun assessing floating technology for deeper waters as an option for later phases.

08 · 1888 – 2025

風機巨型化：為什麼越做越大？Ever-larger turbines: why bigger?

背後是簡單的物理：風機能擷取的功率與葉輪掃風面積成正比（也就是直徑的平方），並與風速的三次方成正比；而越高的地方風越強、越穩。葉片加長一倍，掃風面積就變成四倍；塔架拉高，風速也跟著提高。

The physics is simple: the power a turbine can capture scales with the area its rotor sweeps (the square of the diameter) and with the cube of the wind speed — and wind is stronger and steadier higher up. Double the blade length and the swept area quadruples; raise the tower and the wind speed rises too.

1980 年代加州的風機多只有幾十 kW、葉輪十幾公尺；2002 年 Horns Rev 用的是 2 MW、80 公尺；2019 年台灣海洋風場用 6 MW、154 公尺；2023 年 Vestas V236 原型機達 15 MW、236 公尺。2025 年中國東方電氣的 26 MW 離岸風機，葉輪直徑約 310 公尺——將近三個足球場長。

Californian turbines of the 1980s were mostly a few dozen kilowatts with rotors of ten-odd metres; Horns Rev used 2 MW, 80 m machines in 2002; Taiwan's Formosa 1 used 6 MW, 154 m turbines in 2019; the Vestas V236 prototype reached 15 MW and 236 m in 2023. In 2025 Dongfang Electric's 26 MW offshore turbine in China has a rotor about 310 m across — nearly three football pitches.

更大的風機代表同樣容量需要更少的基礎、海纜與吊裝次數，能明顯降低每度電成本；但也帶來葉片運輸、吊裝船隊、材料與可靠度的新挑戰。台灣的離岸風場正是這個趨勢的縮影：從 2017 年的 4 MW 示範機，到 8 MW、9.5 MW，再到海龍與大彰化西北使用的 14 MW 機組。

Bigger turbines mean fewer foundations, cables and lifts for the same capacity, which cuts the cost per kWh — but they also bring new challenges in blade transport, installation vessels, materials and reliability. Taiwan's offshore farms mirror the trend: from 4 MW demonstrators in 2017 to 8 MW and 9.5 MW machines, and 14 MW units at Hai Long and Greater Changhua 2b&4.

09 · 2000 – 2026

台灣之路：從麥寮到台灣海峽Taiwan's path: from Mailiao to the Strait

台灣的風電始於 2000 年：台塑重工在雲林麥寮設置 4 部 660 kW 風機，是台灣第一批商業運轉的風力機組。之後台電與民間業者（如英華威，今達德能源）沿西部海岸陸續設置陸域風場；2015 年台灣陸域風電約 0.65 GW，此後新增有限，2025 年約 0.95 GW——土地、鄰避與景觀議題，讓陸域風場越來越難找地。

Taiwan's wind story began in 2000, when Formosa Heavy Industries installed four 660 kW turbines at Mailiao, Yunlin — the island's first commercial wind turbines. Taipower and private developers (such as InfraVest, today wpd Taiwan) then built onshore farms along the west coast. Onshore capacity reached about 0.65 GW in 2015 and has grown slowly since, to about 0.95 GW in 2025: land, local opposition and landscape concerns make new onshore sites hard to find.

真正的轉折是離岸風電。經濟部 2012 年推出「千架海陸風力機」計畫，採「先示範、次潛力、後區塊」三階段推動：2017 年海洋風場的 2 部 4 MW 示範機併網，是台灣第一次有離岸風電；2019 年海洋風場 128 MW 完工，成為第一座商轉的離岸風場。2018 年的潛力場址遴選與競價，引進沃旭、哥本哈根基礎建設基金（CIP）、達德、海龍等國際開發商與近兆元投資，也帶動水下基礎、塔架、海纜等國產化供應鏈。

Offshore wind was the real turning point. In 2012 the Ministry of Economic Affairs launched its "Thousand Wind Turbines" programme with a three-phase approach — demonstration, then potential zones, then zonal development. Formosa 1's two 4 MW demonstration turbines connected in 2017, Taiwan's first offshore wind power, and the 128 MW Formosa 1 was completed in 2019 as the first commercial offshore farm. The 2018 allocation and auction of potential zones brought in international developers such as Ørsted, Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP), wpd and Hai Long, close to NT$1 trillion of investment, and a local supply chain for foundations, towers and cables.

依本站資料，台灣離岸風電 2025 年底約 3.6 GW（含部分併網中的風場），名列全球第 5、約佔全球離岸容量的 3.9%，也是中國以外亞洲最大的離岸市場。你可以在「台灣即時」看到每一座風場此刻的出力，也能在 3D 地球儀上看台灣的風場一座座出現。

In this site's data Taiwan had about 3.6 GW offshore at the end of 2025 (including farms still being connected) — fifth in the world, about 3.9% of global offshore capacity, and the largest offshore market in Asia outside China. The Taiwan live section shows what each farm is producing right now, and the 3D globe shows Taiwan's farms appearing one by one.

為什麼台灣需要風電？用數據破除迷思Why Taiwan needs wind — and the myths, busted with data

台灣約 96% 的能源仰賴進口，自產只有約 4.2%（2024，經濟部能源署）。風是台灣少數能「自己生產」的能源——發展風電可以提升能源自主、減少對進口燃料價格波動的曝險，同時減碳、帶動國產化供應鏈與就業。

Taiwan imports about 96% of its energy; only about 4.2% is home-produced (2024, MOEA Energy Administration). Wind is one of the few energy sources Taiwan can make itself — developing it boosts energy self-sufficiency, cuts exposure to imported-fuel price swings, reduces carbon and builds a local supply chain and jobs.

❌「沒風就沒電」❌ “No wind means no power”

台灣冬季東北季風最強，離岸風電正好在冬天發最多；太陽能則在夏天最強。兩者季節互補，加上台電以多元電源與儲能即時調度，並不是「靠單一來源」。

Taiwan's monsoon is strongest in winter — exactly when offshore wind peaks — while solar peaks in summer. The two complement each other, and Taipower balances a diverse mix plus storage in real time; the grid never relies on a single source.

❌「風電很貴」❌ “Wind power is expensive”

從示範／遴選階段約 5.8 元/度，到 2018 年競價的 2.2–2.5 元/度，再到區塊開發改由企業以綠電購售合約（CPPA）承購、不再領政府躉購，離岸風電成本一路大幅下降。

From about NT$5.8/kWh in the demonstration/selection phase, to NT$2.2–2.5/kWh in the 2018 auction, to zonal development where companies buy the power through corporate PPAs instead of a government feed-in tariff, offshore wind costs have fallen sharply.

❌「風機殺死很多鳥」❌ “Turbines kill lots of birds”

依國際研究估算，風機造成的鳥類死亡遠低於家貓、建築玻璃等日常因素。台灣離岸風場另設有鳥類與生態監測，必要時停機以降低衝擊。

International studies estimate that wind turbines kill far fewer birds than domestic cats or building glass. Taiwan's offshore farms also run bird and ecology monitoring and shut down when needed to reduce impact.

🤔 大家常問🤔 Frequently asked

Q沒風的時候不就沒電了？No wind means no power, right?
風電是電力組合的一員，不是唯一來源。台電會用燃氣、水力、太陽能與儲能即時互補調度；各電源也有互補性——白天太陽能強、夜晚與冬季風力強，台灣冬季的東北季風正好補上夏季太陽能的空檔。Wind is one part of the power mix, not the only source. Taipower balances gas, hydro, solar and storage in real time, and sources complement each other — solar is strong by day, wind at night and in winter; Taiwan's winter monsoon fills the gap left by summer solar.
Q風機會不會很吵？Are turbines noisy?
離岸風機位在外海數公里到數十公里處，聲音傳不到岸上。陸域風機的運轉聲在數百公尺外就降到接近環境背景音，且受噪音管制法規約束。Offshore turbines sit kilometres to tens of kilometres out at sea; the sound doesn't reach shore. Onshore turbines drop to near-background levels a few hundred metres away and are subject to noise regulations.
Q颱風來會不會被吹倒？Can a typhoon knock them down?
台灣的風機依國際與在地耐風規範設計，能承受強颱等級的風速；風速過高時葉片會「順槳」並自動停機保護機組，而不是硬扛強風。Turbines here are designed to international and local standards to withstand severe-typhoon winds. When the wind is too strong the blades “feather” and the turbine shuts down automatically to protect itself rather than fighting the gale.
Q離岸風電很貴，會讓電費變高嗎？Is offshore wind expensive — will bills rise?
早期示範與躉購階段費率較高，但已轉為「競價」開發，得標價格大幅下降、趨近市場行情。長期而言，自產綠電能降低對進口燃料價格波動的依賴，有助能源自主。Early demonstration and feed-in phases were pricier, but development moved to competitive auctions and prices fell sharply toward market rates. In the long run, home-grown green power reduces exposure to imported-fuel price swings and supports energy independence.
Q會影響漁業和海洋生態嗎？Does it affect fisheries and marine life?
開發前須通過環境影響評估，並推動漁業共生與長期生態監測。部分研究也觀察到，風機的水下基座有類似「人工魚礁」的聚魚效果。Projects must pass an environmental impact assessment and pursue fishery co-existence and long-term monitoring. Some studies even find that turbine foundations act like artificial reefs that attract fish.

名詞小辭典Glossary

裝置容量Installed capacityMW · GW
風機或風場在理想風況下的最大輸出功率。1 GW = 1,000 MW = 100 萬 kW。The maximum power a turbine or farm can deliver in ideal wind. 1 GW = 1,000 MW = 1 million kW.
發電量Energy generatedkWh ·「度」
功率 × 時間。1 MW 的風機滿載運轉 1 小時＝1,000 度電。Power × time. A 1 MW turbine running flat out for one hour produces 1,000 kWh.
容量因數Capacity factor
一段期間的實際發電量 ÷（裝置容量 × 時數）。陸域約 25–35%，離岸約 35–50%。Actual energy over a period ÷ (capacity × hours). Roughly 25–35% onshore and 35–50% offshore.
可用率（本站）Availability (this site)
即時頁的「可用率」＝此刻出力 ÷ 裝置容量，是會隨風況變動的瞬間值，不是年平均的容量因數。On the live pages, "availability" = current output ÷ capacity: an instantaneous value that moves with the wind, not the annual capacity factor.
陸域／離岸／浮動式Onshore / offshore / floating
蓋在陸地、海上固定式基礎、或海上漂浮平台的風機。本站國際資料的離岸含潮間帶與近岸（GWEC 口徑）。Turbines on land, on fixed foundations at sea, or on floating platforms. Offshore in this site's global data includes intertidal and nearshore farms (GWEC convention).
併網與商轉Grid connection & COD
併網是風機開始把電送進電網；商業運轉（COD）是整座風場完成測試、正式營運。兩者常相隔數月到一年以上。Grid connection is when turbines start feeding power to the grid; commercial operation (COD) is when the whole farm has passed testing and officially starts operating — often months to a year or more later.
躉購與競價Feed-in tariff & auctions
躉購（FIT）：政府以固定費率長期收購電力；競價：開發商以報價競爭開發權，價低者得。Feed-in tariff (FIT): the state buys power at a fixed rate for many years. Auction: developers bid for the right to build and the lowest price wins.
企業購電合約Corporate PPACPPA
企業直接向風場長期購買綠電，例如台積電與沃旭為大彰化 2b&4 簽下的 920 MW、20 年合約。A company buys green power directly from a farm under a long-term contract — e.g. TSMC's 20-year deal with Ørsted for all 920 MW of Greater Changhua 2b&4.
單樁／套管式基礎Monopile / jacket
單樁是打入海床的一根大鋼管；套管式是三或四支腳的鋼構架，適合較深或地質較複雜的海域。A monopile is one large steel tube driven into the seabed; a jacket is a three- or four-legged steel lattice for deeper water or harder ground.
葉輪直徑／輪轂高度Rotor diameter / hub height
葉輪直徑是葉片掃過圓的直徑；輪轂高度是轉軸中心離地（海）面的高度。Rotor diameter is the diameter of the circle the blades sweep; hub height is the height of the rotor's centre above ground or sea level.
切入／切出風速、順槳Cut-in / cut-out, feathering
風速約 3 m/s 開始發電（切入）；約 25 m/s 以上會把葉片轉成順風角度（順槳）並停機保護（切出）。Turbines start generating at about 3 m/s (cut-in); above about 25 m/s they turn the blades edge-on to the wind (feathering) and stop (cut-out).
備轉容量率Operating reserve margin
（供電能力 − 尖峰負載）÷ 尖峰負載。台電以 10%、6%、3% 等門檻分成綠、黃、橘、紅燈號。(Supply capacity − peak load) ÷ peak load. Taipower uses thresholds such as 10%, 6% and 3% for its green, yellow, orange and red lights.

  • 加拿大亞伯達：AESO「Current Supply Demand」報表，各風場約 1 分鐘的即時淨出力。© 2026 The Independent System Operator（AESO）. All rights reserved；依 AESO 網站條款供非商業與教育用途，數值未經修改。
  • Alberta: AESO "Current Supply Demand" report, each wind asset's net output about a minute old. © 2026 THE INDEPENDENT SYSTEM OPERATOR ("ISO"). All rights reserved; used for non-commercial, educational purposes under AESO's website terms, values unmodified.
  • 加拿大安大略：IESO「Generators Output and Capability」報表，各風場每小時出力。Copyright © 2004-2022 Independent Electricity System Operator, all rights reserved. This information is subject to the Terms of Use set out in the IESO's website (www.ieso.ca).
  • Ontario: IESO "Generators Output and Capability" report, each wind farm's hourly output. Copyright © 2004-2022 Independent Electricity System Operator, all rights reserved. This information is subject to the Terms of Use set out in the IESO's website (www.ieso.ca).
  • 由 GitHub Actions 約每 2 小時抓取。電網只給機組代碼，本站以 AEMO 登錄清單與 IESO「Transmission-Connected Generation」人工核對對應到風場；對不到的機組只計入電網總量，一個機組涵蓋數座風場時依容量比例估算並標示。詳見 即時資料來源評估。
  • Fetched about every 2 hours by GitHub Actions. The grids only publish unit codes, so units are matched to farms using AEMO's registration list and IESO's "Transmission-Connected Generation" page, checked by hand; unmatched units only count toward the grid total, and a unit covering several farms is split by capacity and labelled as an estimate.   • 風場層級：Global Energy Monitor「全球風電追蹤」（2025-02，CC BY 4.0）為主，加上維基百科各國風場清單、WRI Global Power Plant Database 等精選風場，以及日本 NEDO／windfarm.work 清單：營運中逾 1.5 萬座、規劃與興建中近 8,000 案，座標多為概略位置；風場照片與簡介於瀏覽時即時查詢維基百科。國界：Natural Earth 1:50m（公有領域）。
  • Farm level: mainly the Global Energy Monitor Global Wind Power Tracker (Feb 2025, CC BY 4.0), plus curated farms from Wikipedia lists, the WRI Global Power Plant Database and others, and the NEDO / windfarm.work lists for Japan: 15,000+ operating farms and nearly 8,000 pipeline projects, mostly with approximate coordinates; farm photos and summaries are looked up live from Wikipedia.
